Pricing & Scope

What it costs and what's included

How much does a project cost?

Brand identity starts at $1,800, websites at $2,500, packaging and print at $650, merch graphics at $450 per design, and monthly social content at $950 per month.

Those are genuine starting points, not teaser rates. After the free consult you receive a written flat-rate quote for your specific scope, and that approved number is what you pay.

Why flat rates instead of hourly billing?

Hourly billing punishes you for asking questions and rewards us for working slowly. A flat rate means you can send a second round of feedback without watching a meter run, and we can spend the time a problem actually needs.

What happens if the scope changes mid-project?

We tell you before anything is built. Added deliverables get a written add-on price you approve in writing, so an invoice never arrives with line items you haven't already agreed to.

Do you offer bundles for multiple services?

Yes. Combining identity with a website, or a brand with a merch capsule, is quoted as a bundle at a lower combined rate than booking each separately. It's also faster, because discovery only happens once.

Is the consultation really free?

It is — 30 minutes by phone or video, no obligation. You'll get an honest read on what to prioritize and a written summary afterward, whether or not you hire us.

Timeline & Process

How the work actually runs

How long will my project take?

Brand identity is usually 2–3 weeks, websites 3–5 weeks, print and packaging 1–3 weeks, and merch graphics 1–2 weeks — measured from kickoff, assuming feedback comes back within about two business days.

Can you hit a hard deadline?

Often, yes — but we'll only say yes if it's real. Bring your launch, opening or drop date to the consult and we'll confirm feasibility before you pay anything. If a rush schedule is required, the rush fee is quoted up front.

How many revisions are included?

Two full rounds on every project. Because we align on direction before design starts, most clients use the first round for refinements and barely touch the second. Extra rounds are available at a flat per-round rate.

Who will I actually be working with?

The designer building your project. There's no account manager layer and no handoff to a junior after you sign — the person on your consult call is the person doing the work.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your goals, your audience, your deadline, and any examples of work you love or hate. Once the quote is approved, a 50% deposit books your production slot and a short questionnaire kicks the project off.

What if I don't like any of the concepts?

It's rare, because direction is agreed before design begins — but if the first presentation misses, tell us plainly. We'll regroup on direction and present again within the included revision structure rather than billing you for our miss.

Files, Payment & Logistics

The practical details

Do I own the final files and artwork?

Completely, on final payment. You get vector logos (AI, SVG, EPS), raster exports (PNG, JPG), print-ready PDFs, editable layouts, a fonts and licensing list, and your brand guidelines. Nothing is withheld and you're never locked into us for future edits.

How do payments work?

A 50% deposit books your slot and the remaining 50% is due at delivery. Monthly retainers bill at the start of each month. We accept card, bank transfer and major digital payment methods, and every project is covered by a simple written agreement.

Do you work with businesses outside Los Angeles?

Yes. We're an LA studio and enjoy meeting local clients in person, but about half our projects are remote for clients across the country. Video calls, shared presentation links and email keep the process identical.

Can you fix or refresh a brand I already have?

Yes, and often that's the smarter spend. If your existing mark carries recognition, we modernize around it instead of scrapping it. The free consult includes an honest audit of what's worth keeping.

Do you handle printing and production too?

We design and prep production-ready files, then coordinate directly with your printer or manufacturer. If you don't have one, we'll recommend vetted LA vendors we've worked with — you contract them directly, so there's no markup on production.

What kind of support do I get after launch?

Thirty days of support on every project — small fixes, questions, file format requests and a walkthrough so you know how to use what you've been given. After that, ongoing support is available hourly or on a retainer.

How soon can you start?

We hold a limited number of production slots, and availability typically opens within one to two weeks. Call (424) 355-4446 for this month's real availability — we'll tell you straight if we're booked.

Still have a question?

Call the studio directly or send it over — you'll get a real answer, not a sales pitch.
