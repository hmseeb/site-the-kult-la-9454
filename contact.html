Before You Send

Quick answers

How fast will I hear back?

Within one business day, every time. Calls placed between 9am and 6pm PT on weekdays are usually answered live.

Is the quote really free and fixed?

Yes. The consult and the written quote cost nothing, and the flat rate you approve is the price of the project. Any scope changes are priced and approved before work continues.

What if I'm not sure what I need yet?

Choose "Not sure yet — I'd like advice" on the form. We'll audit what you have and tell you what's worth fixing first, in priority order, before recommending anything.

Do you take on rush projects?

Often — call rather than emailing so we can check availability live. If a rush schedule works, the rush fee is quoted up front with the rest of the scope.
