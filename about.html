About the Studio

Independent, opinionated, and easy to reach

The Kult LA is a small Los Angeles design studio built on a simple idea: independent brands deserve agency-quality work without the agency runaround, the padded hours, or the account manager who forwards your emails to someone you've never met.

Our Story

Nine years of building LA brands

The studio started the way a lot of good things in Los Angeles do — one designer, a laptop, and a friend's restaurant that needed a menu by Friday. That menu turned into signage, then packaging, then a full rebrand, then referrals from every operator who walked through the door.

Nine years and more than 180 projects later, the work spans streetwear labels, coffee shops, recording artists, fitness studios and venture-backed founders. What hasn't changed is the model: you work directly with the person designing your project, you know the price before anyone opens Illustrator, and you get a straight answer when something isn't worth doing.

We're deliberately small. That means we take on a limited number of projects at a time, and it means your deadline is a commitment rather than a queue position.

How We Work

Four things we refuse to compromise on

These aren't slogans — they're the reasons clients send their friends here.

Fixed pricing

Every quote is a flat rate tied to a deliverable list. If the scope grows, we tell you the new number before we touch it.

Deadlines that hold

Dates go in the scope, and we build in buffer instead of promising fantasy timelines. If a date is at risk, you hear it from us first.

Plain English

No jargon walls. We explain what a decision does for your business — reach, clarity, conversions — and let you make the call.

You own everything

Source files, fonts list, print exports, guidelines — all handed over on final payment. No hostage files, no forced retainers.
