FAQ

Questions we get asked every week

Still curious? Call (424) 355-4446 and ask directly.

How much does a project cost?

Brand identity starts at $1,800, websites at $2,500, packaging and print at $650, merch graphics at $450 per design, and monthly social content at $950. Every project gets a written flat-rate quote before any work begins, so the number you approve is the number you pay.

How long will my project take?

Most identity projects run 2–3 weeks and websites 3–5 weeks from kickoff, assuming feedback comes back within a couple of days. Print and merch work is usually 1–3 weeks. If you have a hard deadline — a launch, an event, a drop — tell us on the consult and we'll confirm whether we can hit it before you commit.

Do you work with businesses outside Los Angeles?

Yes. We're based in LA and love meeting local clients in person, but roughly half our work is remote for clients across the country. Everything runs over video calls, shared decks and email — the process is identical.

How many revisions are included?

Two full revision rounds are built into every quote. In practice that's plenty — we align on direction before design starts, so revisions are refinements rather than restarts. Additional rounds are available at a flat per-round rate quoted up front.

Do I own the final files and artwork?

Completely. On final payment you receive full ownership plus organized source files — vector logos, editable layouts, fonts list, print-ready exports and your brand guidelines. Nothing is held hostage, and you're never locked into us for future edits.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your goals, your audience, your deadline, and any examples of work you like or hate. That's enough for a consult. Once you approve the quote, a 50% deposit books your production slot and we send a short questionnaire to kick things off.

Can you fix or refresh a brand I already have?

Absolutely — refreshes are a big part of what we do. If your logo still has equity we'll modernize around it instead of scrapping it. The free consult includes an honest audit telling you what's worth keeping and what's holding you back.
