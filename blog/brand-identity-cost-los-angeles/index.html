Brand Identity Cost in Los Angeles: What to Expect

Brand identity projects start from $1,800 with a 2–3 week turnaround.

By The Kult LA · Updated October 16, 2026

How much does a brand identity cost in Los Angeles?

Brand identity projects start from $1,800 with a 2–3 week turnaround.

What does a brand identity project include?

A brand identity project delivers a complete visual system — not just a logo file. We define the positioning, then build the marks, type, color and rules that keep you consistent everywhere.

The marks include primary, secondary and submark logos.

How long does a brand identity project take?

Most identity projects run 2–3 weeks and websites 3–5 weeks from kickoff, assuming feedback comes back within a couple of days. Print and merch work is usually 1–3 weeks. If you have a hard deadline — a launch, an event, a drop — tell us on the consult and we'll confirm whether we can hit it before you commit.

What do you get at the end of a brand identity project?

On final payment you receive full ownership plus organized source files — vector logos, editable layouts, fonts list, print-ready exports and your brand guidelines. Nothing is held hostage, and you're never locked into us for future edits.
